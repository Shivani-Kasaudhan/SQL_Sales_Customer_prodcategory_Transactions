This repository provides data for analyzing customer behavior, transaction trends, and product performance for a retail store. The dataset consists of three interconnected files: Customer.csv, Transactions.csv, and Pro_Cat_Info.csv. These files offer rich insights into customer demographics, purchase details, and product hierarchies. Below is an overview of the datasets and the types of questions that can be addressed:

1. Customer.csv
Description: Contains demographic information about customers, including their age, gender, and city of residence.
Key Columns:
Customer_ID: Unique customer identifier.
Age, Gender: Demographic details.
City: Location of the customer.
Example Questions:
What is the distribution of customers by gender?
Which cities have the highest number of customers?
How does age distribution vary across cities?
2. Transactions.csv
Description: Includes detailed information about each transaction made by customers, such as products purchased, quantities, and transaction channels.
Key Columns:
Transaction_ID, Customer_ID: Transaction and customer references.
Product_ID, Quantity, Price: Product details and purchase data.
Transaction_Date, Channel: Date and mode of purchase.
Example Questions:
What is the total revenue generated by each sales channel?
Which products are most frequently purchased, and in what quantities?
What is the time-based trend of transactions (e.g., daily, monthly)?
3. Pro_Cat_Info.csv
Description: Provides the product hierarchy, linking product categories to their subcategories.
Key Columns:
Category, SubCategory: Product categorization.
Product_ID: Reference for individual products.
Example Questions:
Which product subcategories are the most popular in terms of sales?
How does revenue vary across different product categories?
What is the percentage contribution of each subcategory to its parent category?
Analytical Insights:
With these datasets, analysts can answer a wide range of business-critical questions, such as:

Customer Behavior: What is the profile of high-value customers? Which age group contributes the most to revenue?
Sales Performance: Which products or categories generate the highest revenue? Which channels drive the most sales?
Operational Trends: What is the return rate by category or subcategory? Are there seasonal patterns in purchases?
These insights can help retailers optimize marketing strategies, improve product assortments, and enhance overall business performance.
